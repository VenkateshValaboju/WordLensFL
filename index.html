Ephemeral
/ɪˈfem.ər.əl/
adjective
Lasting for a very short time; transitory. Often used to describe things that exist only briefly before disappearing.
✦ IELTS Tip Common in academic reading passages about nature and technology. Antonyms: permanent, enduring. Useful in Writing Task 2 essays on social trends.
