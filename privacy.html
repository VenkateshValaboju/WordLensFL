Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

WordLens is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over it.

1. Who We Are

WordLens is an AI-powered English dictionary Chrome extension built for IELTS, CAT, TOEFL students and avid readers. The extension is developed and maintained by Venkatesh Valaboju, India.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: venkateshvalaboju@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the service:

  • Email address — collected when you sign in with Google, used to identify your account
  • Name and profile picture — collected from Google Sign-In, displayed in your account
  • Words you look up — processed to generate definitions, not stored permanently
  • Words you save — stored locally on your device in Chrome storage
  • Subscription status — stored to verify your plan (Free or Pro)
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card or UPI details

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and manage your account
  • To provide word definitions and AI-powered lookup results
  • To track your free AI lookup usage (limited to 20 for free accounts)
  • To verify your subscription status and grant Pro access
  • To process payments via Razorpay

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the WordLens service.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not track which websites you visit
  • We do not record your browsing history
  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not show ads or share data with advertisers
  • We do not sell your data to any third party

5. Third-Party Services

WordLens uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay — for payment processing and subscription management. Razorpay Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI — for AI-generated definitions (words not found in standard dictionary). Only the word itself is sent, no personal data. OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Workers — our backend API runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — for secure database storage of user accounts. Supabase Privacy Policy

6. Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored securely in Supabase (Singapore region). Passwords are never stored — we use Google OAuth for authentication. All communication between the extension and our servers uses HTTPS encryption.

Your saved vocabulary list is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API and is not transmitted to our servers.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Cancel your subscription at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at venkateshvalaboju@gmail.com

9. Children's Privacy

WordLens is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of WordLens after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
